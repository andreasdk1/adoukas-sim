Who is responsible

Analytics (only with your consent)

With your consent, this site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are read. Until you click Accept, nothing from Google is loaded and no analytics cookie is set; if you click Reject or do nothing, it stays that way.

  • What is collected: pages viewed, how you arrived (referrer), approximate location (country or city, derived from your IP address, which Google Analytics 4 does not store), device and browser type, and anonymous identifiers in the cookies _ga and _ga_EMZ0MM9TYB.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and the consent required for cookies under the Slovenian Electronic Communications Act).
  • Recipient: Google Ireland Limited, with processing by Google LLC in the USA. The transfer to the USA relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, under which Google LLC is certified.
  • Settings: Google signals and advertising features are off, and the data is used for nothing but these statistics.
  • Retention: analytics data is kept for 2 months; the cookies expire after at most 2 years.
  • Changing your mind: use Cookie settings (also at the bottom of every page). Withdrawing consent deletes the analytics cookies; it does not affect what was collected before.

Your choice itself is stored in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) so the site can remember it; it is never sent anywhere.

Contact form

If you send a message through the contact form, your name, email address, the service area you choose and your message are sent to me through Formspree, Inc. (USA), which forwards them by email. I use them only to answer your enquiry and, if we work together, for that work.

  • Legal basis: steps you ask for before a possible contract, and my legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).
  • Retention: enquiries that lead nowhere are deleted after at most 12 months; correspondence about a project is kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Transfer: Formspree processes the data in the USA, under the safeguards set out in its data processing agreement.
